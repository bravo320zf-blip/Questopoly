ARCHIVES OF LEGEND

✦ Objective

Be the last Hero standing! Bankrupt your rivals by capturing locations, upgrading them to Taverns, and hoarding Gold. If you run out of Gold and Properties to sell, you are eliminated.

✦ The World

  • Day & Night: The world cycles every 10 rounds.
    Day: Standard visibility.
    Night: Fog rolls in, obscuring vision. Monsters become more aggressive (Harder "Skirmish" cards).
  • Movement: Roll 2d6 to move. Landing on Start (The Inn) or passing it recharges your abilities and grants 200G.
  • The Dungeon (Jail): If arrested, you must skip a turn. Some abilities (like Rogue skills) can help you escape or trap others.

✦ Stats

Your ability to succeed in checks depends on your stats:

  • STR (Strength): Used for brute force, smashing obstacles, and heavy weapons.
  • DEX (Dexterity): Used for dodging, traps, acrobatics, and ranged weapons.
  • INT (Intelligence): Used for magic, puzzles, and resisting spells.

Secondary Stats:

MOV | GF | RES | HP | DODGE | SURGE
  • MOV (Movement): Bonus spaces added to your roll.
  • GF (Gold Find): Extra gold found in chests.
  • RES (Resistance): Reduced gold lost from taxes/theft.
  • HP (Max Health): Total Hit Points (Base 10 + STR).
  • DODGE: Chance to avoid attacks (1% per DEX).
  • SURGE: Chance for 3x damage (1% per INT).

✦ Skills

Every Hero has:

  • Active Skill (Slot 1): A powerful ability you can trigger on your turn. Once used, it must be Recharged by passing Start.
  • Passive Trait: A permanent bonus (e.g., discounts, extra movement, immunity).
  • Equipment Skills: Rare and Legendary items grant additional Active Skills.

✦ Status Effects

Polymorph: If turned into a Sheep, you cannot use skills or items, and your stats are reduced to 1. Surviving a turn cures you.

Stun/Jail: You lose your turn.

✦ Capturing Land

When you land on an empty location, you face an Encounter Card. You must roll dice equal to your specific Stat (STR/DEX/INT) against a Target Number (TN).

  • Success: You capture the land and set up a Camp. You choose which Stat defends it.
  • Failure: You pay a fine to the Treasury.

✦ Enemy Territory

Landing on a rival's land offers a choice:

  1. Pay Rent: Simple and safe.
  2. Siege (Attack): Roll your stats against the land's Defense.
    • Win: You downgrade the building or destroy it. If the owner hired Guards, you kill one Guard instead.
    • Lose: You pay Double Rent to the owner.

Note: Buildings upgraded to Taverns (Level 2) are harder to siege.

✦ World Events

Random events occur periodically. These can be weather effects (Blizzards slowing movement), economic booms, or visiting Merchants.

✦ Dungeons

Rarely, a mythical Dungeon may appear attached to a specific location (e.g., The Void Edge or Demon Gate).

  • Entering: You must be at the entrance tile to enter. Dungeons are High Difficulty areas.
  • Movement: Inside a dungeon, you move 1 tile per turn automatically.
  • Combat: Every step triggers a dangerous encounter. If you fail, you are cast out to the Inn.
  • The Boss: At the end lies a Boss. Defeating them grants massive Treasure loot. Failing expels you from the dungeon.
  • Ambush: Beware! Rolling a 6 while inside a dungeon may summon the Boss immediately to your location.

✦ Shops & Items

Merchants: Found at Start, Ports, and via Roaming Events. You can buy Equipment (Stat boosts) or Scrolls (One-time use spells).

Selling: You can sell items back to any merchant for half price. (Long-press an item in the shop menu).

Inventory Limit: You can carry up to 12 items.

✦ The Treasury

Fines and Taxes are paid into the Kingdom's Treasury. If you land on the Capital City (Free Parking), you win the entire pot!

✦ Etiquette

  • Turn Timer: You have 15 seconds to take your turn (45s in Shops). If you run out of time, your turn is skipped.
  • AFK Penalty: Missing 3 turns in a row results in being kicked from the game.
  • Reconnecting: If you disconnect, reload the page. The game state is saved on the server.

✦ Communication

Use the Global Chat in the lobby or Room Chat in-game. You can also right-click (or long-press) a player in the Leaderboard to send a Direct Message.

HOW TO PLAY

🎲 Core Stats & Mechanics

Roll dice to move. Goal: Acquire territory, build wealth, defeat bosses.

  • STR (Strength): Increases Attack & Max HP (HP = 10 + STR).
  • DEX (Dexterity): Increases Attack & Dodge (1% per point).
  • INT (Intelligence): Increases Attack & Mana Surge (1% chance for 3x Damage).

Secondary Stats (Layout):

Row 1: MOV | GF | RES
Row 2: HP | DODGE | SURGE
  • MOV (Movement): Adds to your movement roll. (Increased by Boots & Skills)
  • GF (Gold Find): Bonus gold from chests. (Increased by Items & Traits)
  • RES (Resistance): Reduces gold lost. (Increased by Armor)
  • HP (Max Health): Total hit points (Base 10). (Increased by STR)
  • DODGE (Evasion): % chance to avoid hits. (Increased by DEX)
  • SURGE (Mana): % chance for 3x damage. (Increased by INT)

⛺ Properties & Territory

Claiming Land:

  • Win a battle on an unowned tile to automatically claim it as a Campsite (Level 1) - it's FREE!
  • Landing on unowned property triggers an encounter card - pass the skill check to claim it
  • Upgrade your Campsite to a Tavern (Level 2) for increased rent income

Enemy Territory:

  • Pay rent OR attempt a Siege to capture it
  • Guards increase defense and rent cost
  • Failed sieges = gold penalty paid to the owner

⚔️ Combat System

Overworld Battles: Land on spaces to encounter monsters. Win to automatically claim the territory!

Battle Mechanics:

  • Choose STR, DEX, or INT to attack each round
  • Roll dice based on your stat value - each die that rolls 4+ = 1 hit
  • Most hits wins the round!
  • Dodge: Your DEX gives you a chance to completely avoid enemy attacks (1% per DEX point)
  • Win enough rounds to defeat the enemy
  • Skills can be used once per battle for powerful effects
  • Mana Surge: Your INT gives abilities a chance to deal TRIPLE damage (1% per INT point)
  • Fleeing = gold penalty

🏰 Dungeons

Dungeons appear randomly on the map (glowing sword icon). Enter to face increasingly difficult monster waves!

  • Wave System: Defeat multiple enemies in sequence
  • Boss Battles: Final wave features a powerful boss
  • Rewards: Epic loot and gold for completing dungeons
  • Death: Losing in a dungeon kicks you out (no property loss)
  • Dungeon Types: Demon Gate, Void Edge, Vampire Mansion, Wyvern Peak - each with unique bosses

✨ Skills & Abilities

Skill Bar: Bottom of screen shows your equipped skills (slots 1-6)

  • Class Skills: Unlocked from your chosen race/class at game start
  • Equipment Skills: Granted by equipped items with special abilities
  • Managing Skills: Right-click skill slots to assign/swap abilities
  • Cooldowns: Most skills recharge after each turn or battle
  • Tooltips: Hover over skills to see details

💎 Items & Equipment

Treasure Chests: Land on chest spaces for random loot

Rarity Tiers: Common → Rare → Epic → Legendary → Ancient

  • Ancient Items: 10% chance to upgrade Legendary equipment with powerful unique abilities
  • Equipment Slots: Head, Body, Main Hand, Off Hand, Feet, Hands, Rings (2), Amulet, Back
  • Stat Bonuses: Gear increases STR/DEX/INT plus secondary stats like Movement, Gold Find, Resistance
  • Scrolls: Consumable items with one-time powerful effects

🪤 Traps

Players with the Skeleton Key passive can set traps at the Dungeon (Jail) tile:

  • Cost: 50 gold to set/overwrite a trap
  • When opponents land on the Dungeon, your trap triggers!
  • Effects: Steal gold, damage stats, or teleport enemies
  • Strategic positioning for maximum chaos!

🌟 Random Events

Special events can spawn at the start of turns:

  • Merchants: Purchase unique items from traveling vendors
  • Weather: Blizzard (slow movement), Gold Rain (bonus gold per move), Fog (random skill choices)
  • Tax Collector: Reduces your movement to 1 die for 3 turns
  • Hoard Dragon: Steal gold from all players!
  • Events last for a limited duration

🎯 Special Mechanics

  • Polymorph: Get turned into a sheep! Movement = 1 for one turn
  • Sanctuary: Clerics can set a respawn point. Death teleports you there
  • Bonus Loot: 1 in 6 chance to find hidden treasure after claiming property
  • Treasury: Landing on the Park space collects the accumulated pot
  • Inn Shop: Buy items at the start tile

👥 Multiplayer

  • Create Game: From main menu, set room name and player count
  • Join Game: Enter room code or select from available games list
  • Turn Timer: 90 seconds per turn (configurable by host)
  • AI Players: Fill empty slots with AI opponents
  • Chat: Communicate with other players during the game
  • Elimination: Lose all properties = you're out!

💡 Pro Tips

  • Balance stat investments - don't neglect any stat!
  • Upgrade properties early for consistent income
  • Save gold for dungeons - big rewards await!
  • Equip items that match your playstyle (combat vs. economy)
  • Night encounters are tough - prepare before nightfall
  • Watch out for other players' traps at the Dungeon
